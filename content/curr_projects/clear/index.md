CLEAR (Carbon-Loaded Electrochemical Super Retention) is an EPA-funded project exploring nutrient reduction and recovery from agricultural and urban runoff. Carbon-based treatment systems are being developed to improve water quality and support nutrient reuse.

[Public EPA project description](https://www.epa.gov/gulfofamerica/overview-innovative-solutions-projects-funded-fy26).
