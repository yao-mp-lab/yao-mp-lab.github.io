The MSCE Lab (“Megascale”) in the Zachry Department of Civil and Environmental Engineering at Texas A&M University is recruiting **one funded Ph.D. student to start in Fall 2027**. The research will focus on computational electrokinetic flow in water treatment, resource recovery, and energy systems, using computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations and physics-based AI methods.

Our related [EPA-funded CLEAR project](/curr_projects/#project-clear) explores nutrient reduction and recovery from agricultural and urban runoff, connecting water treatment with resource reuse.

## What do we expect from you?

- A strong background in CFD and numerical methods is required.
- Programming proficiency in Fortran, C, or C++ is required.
- Prior experience with physics-based AI applications is strongly preferred.
- A master’s degree in a relevant field is highly preferred. Exceptional candidates without a master’s degree will also be considered.
- Relevant fields include computational and applied mathematics, mechanical engineering, civil/environmental engineering, and related disciplines.
- Demonstrated research experience and preparation in these areas will be valued more than the specific degree.

## How to apply

Interested candidates are encouraged to contact [Dr. Yao at yyao@tamu.edu](mailto:yyao@tamu.edu). Please use the subject line **“2027 Fall Ph.D. RA [Your Name]”**, replacing `[Your Name]` with your name.

Please submit **a single PDF file** containing:

1. A one-page cover letter describing your background, research expertise, and interests in CFD and AI.
2. Your curriculum vitae.
3. Unofficial transcripts highlighting coursework related to CFD and numerical methods.

Formal applications through the graduate school system will be requested from strong candidates. Candidates remain subject to departmental and graduate admissions review. An RA position is not guaranteed until formal admission and an official offer are issued, and all applicants must satisfy Texas A&M graduate program admission requirements.

**Application consideration begins immediately and continues until the position is filled.**

For undergraduate research, graduate study with external support, and visiting opportunities, see [Join the lab](/join/).
